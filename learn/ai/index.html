How do I learn AI and machine learning?

AI is the most crowded field to learn and the easiest one to waste time in. The shortest honest path is a canonical textbook, one code-first course that has you training models in the first week, and the primary-source talks from the labs actually building frontier models.

Where to start

Start with Andrej Karpathy's Neural Networks: Zero to Hero to build a GPT from scratch, pair it with Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville's Deep Learning for the theory, then fast.ai's Practical Deep Learning for Coders to ship something. For reinforcement learning, OpenAI's Spinning Up in Deep RL is the standard entry point.

Questions people ask before starting

Do I need a degree to work in AI?

No for engineering roles, usually yes for research roles at frontier labs, where a PhD or a research fellowship (Anthropic Fellows, Google PhD Fellowship, NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship) is the common route.

What is the best free way to learn deep learning?

Karpathy's Zero to Hero series plus the free Deep Learning book and fast.ai; all three are free and are the most frequently recommended resources on Hacker News over the past decade.

Which math do I need first?

Linear algebra, probability and multivariable calculus at an undergraduate level; Mathematics for Machine Learning (Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong) is the free text written for exactly this.

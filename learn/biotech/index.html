How do I learn biotechnology and synthetic biology?

Biotech now spans wet-lab biology, genomics and increasingly machine learning. The best way in is a strong molecular biology foundation, then a lab or a program that puts you at a bench.

Where to start

Start with MIT's 7.00x Introduction to Biology and Stanford's molecular genetics lectures, EMBL-EBI's free bioinformatics training, and iGEM or BioBuilder if you want to build something. Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory's courses and the Broad, HHMI and Arc Institute programs are the research routes.

Questions people ask before starting

Can I work in biotech without a PhD?

Yes in engineering, data and manufacturing roles; discovery research still expects a PhD, which is why fellowships and research training programs matter here.

Where do I learn CRISPR and gene editing?

Jennifer Doudna's and the Broad Institute's lectures for the science; Addgene's guides and iGEM for hands-on; Mammoth Biosciences and Profluent for where the field is going.

Which accelerators focus on biotech?

Nucleate Activator, IndieBio, LabCentral and Y Combinator all take bio companies; Flagship Pioneering creates them in-house.

How this list is made

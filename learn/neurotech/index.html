How do I get into neurotechnology and brain-computer interfaces?

Neurotech sits between neuroscience, electrical engineering and machine learning. A good foundation in neural computation plus hands-on signal work gets you further than any one of them alone.

Where to start

Start with MIT's 9.40 Introduction to Neural Computation and Nancy Kanwisher's 9.13 The Human Brain, then Neuromatch Academy for computational neuroscience and OpenBCI or NeuroTechX for building with real signals. Neuralink's, Synchron's and Paradromics' technical updates show the state of implants.

Questions people ask before starting

What background do BCI companies want?

Electrical or biomedical engineering, neuroscience with programming, or machine learning with signal processing; Neuralink, Synchron and Paradromics hire across all three.

How do I get hands-on without a lab?

An OpenBCI board, NeuroTechX's community projects, Backyard Brains kits and the Brainhack events.

Which programs are the real entry points?

Neuromatch Academy, the Marine Biological Laboratory's neuroscience courses, Cold Spring Harbor's neuroscience programs and the Allen Institute's summer workshops.
